# MP1994 Claim Contract

Paper: Dale T. Mortensen and Christopher A. Pissarides, “Job Creation and Job
Destruction in the Theory of Unemployment,” *Review of Economic Studies* 61 (1994),
397-415.  
Local paper: `Lean4Tutorial/i002_replicate_MP/Mortensen-JobCreationJob-1994.pdf`  
Lean root: `Lean4Tutorial/i002_replicate_MP`

This contract is a paper-to-proof specification. It records equations, model assumptions,
prose results, comparative statics, existence/uniqueness statements, Appendix arguments,
and external or unstated analytic steps. Short source passages are closely transcribed;
formulas are normalized to the paper's intended notation where PDF text extraction is
ambiguous.

## 1. Contract schema and status vocabulary

Every row records:

1. a unique claim ID;
2. printed paper page, section, and equation number;
3. a short exact or close source passage;
4. intended mathematics;
5. explicit assumptions;
6. implicit assumptions;
7. cited or logically required dependencies;
8. claim kind;
9. paper notation;
10. normalized Lean notation;
11. an existing Lean declaration, if any;
12. relation of Lean to paper;
13. green/amber/red adequacy;
14. the smallest additional result needed for green.

Relation codes:

- **Aligned**: the Lean type states the paper claim at essentially the same strength.
- **Weaker** / **Stronger**: Lean proves less / demands more.
- **Conditional**: the key economic premise is a field or theorem hypothesis.
- **Circular**: the claimed conclusion is materially present in the hypotheses.
- **Representational**: the formula is defined or stored, not derived.
- **Missing**: no corresponding declaration.

Grades:

- **Green**: faithful statement and proof, with assumptions explicit.
- **Amber**: useful representation or conditional proof, but not the complete paper claim.
- **Red**: missing, circular, or materially mismatched.
